MILE Lab is part of the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Yeungnam University. We work on learning representations that hold up across modalities — MRI, PET, EEG, fNIRS — and across the subjects, sites and label budgets that make medical machine learning hard in practice.

Our recent work spans multimodal brain-signal foundation models, cross-modality image synthesis, and label-efficient learning under noisy or subjective annotation. The lab opened in 2026 and is recruiting its first students.
